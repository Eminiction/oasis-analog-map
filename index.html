3 · The OASIS outpost

A modular, autonomous base in Bunger Hills, operated remotely and serviced seasonally. It keeps measurements and technology tests running through the polar winter — the same constraints a lunar or Martian base faces: isolation, limited logistics, cold, and no one around to fix failures. Modules are numbered in deployment order, from the first season onward; positions on the Earth map are illustrative.

Data & services: NASA GIBS / Worldview (MODIS Terra, ASTER GDEM) · NASA Moon Trek (LRO WAC, LRO LOLA) · NASA Mars Trek (Viking MDIM, MGS MOLA) · Esri World Imagery basemap · Leaflet (BSD-2). Science context: Andersen et al. 1995, NTRS 19950035178; Doran et al. 1996, Antarctic Science 8(3).
